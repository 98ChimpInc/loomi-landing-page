Frequently Asked Questions

How does Loomi work?

Loomi is an audio-first bedtime story app for children ages 0–6. You pick a story, your child listens to it narrated over a soft lullaby melody, and when the story ends the melody continues until the sleep timer runs out. The phone is meant to go face-down ... there's nothing visual to engage with after you press play.

Why don't I see a screen during playback?

That's intentional. Loomi is built around our core promise: start and forget. Once a story is playing you can place the phone face-down and Loomi takes care of the rest. The dim Dream Mode interface is designed to keep light out of your child's room.

How do subscriptions work?

Loomi offers two subscription plans through the App Store:

  • Loomi Monthly — billed monthly, cancel anytime
  • Loomi Annual — billed yearly, best value

Both plans start with a 14-day free trial. You can cancel anytime before the trial ends and you won't be charged ... on iPhone in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, on Android in the Play Store under Subscriptions.

Subscriptions are managed through the App Store on iPhone and through Google Play on Android. Loomi does not store payment information.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name at the top to open your Apple Account
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Tap Loomi
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription

On Android:

  1. Open the Play Store app
  2. Tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions
  3. Tap Subscriptions, then Loomi
  4. Tap Cancel subscription

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Sign in to the new device with the same App Store or Google Play account and install Loomi. On iPhone, open Settings → Restore Purchases inside the app. On Android your subscription is restored automatically once you are signed in to Google Play.

How do I delete my account?

Inside the Loomi app, open Settings → Account → Delete account, type the confirmation phrase, and confirm with the same provider you signed in with. This works on both iPhone and Android.

What is deleted: your Loomi account and sign-in, every child profile and its preferences, all listening activity, your app settings, any answers you gave to in-app surveys, and the story audio downloaded on that device. This happens immediately and cannot be undone.

What is kept, and for how long: nothing that identifies you. Loomi runs no servers of its own ... your data lives in Google Firebase, and once your account is deleted the records above are gone from it. Anonymous, aggregated usage statistics that can no longer be linked to your account may remain in our analytics. Google keeps its own operational logs of the underlying service under its own retention policies, which we do not control.

Your subscription is separate. Deleting your Loomi account does not cancel it. Cancel it first ... on iPhone in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, on Android in the Play Store under Subscriptions ... or you will continue to be billed.

Prefer email? Write to us at privacy@loomi.kids and we'll handle the deletion within 7 days.

Stories aren't downloading or playing

  • Check your internet connection ... downloads require Wi-Fi or cellular data
  • Make sure your device has enough free storage (each story is approx. 3–5 MB)
  • Try force-quitting Loomi and reopening it
  • If the issue persists, email support@loomi.kids with your phone model and OS version

My child's profile disappeared

Profiles are saved to your Loomi account, not to a single device, so they follow you when you reinstall or switch phones. If a profile is missing, check that you are signed in with the same method you first used ... signing in with Apple and signing in with Google create two separate accounts, each with their own profiles. If it is still missing, email support@loomi.kids and we will help.

Can I use Loomi on a tablet?

Loomi is designed for phones ... iPhone and Android. It installs and runs on an iPad or an Android tablet, but the layout is tuned for a phone screen. Proper tablet support may come in a future release.

Is Loomi safe for my child?

Yes. Loomi is built for kids, with a strict privacy stance:

  • We do not collect personal information from children
  • There are no ads
  • There are no social features
  • There is no in-app messaging
  • Subscription purchases and external links are protected behind a parental gate
