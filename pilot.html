Research pilot

Fourteen nights of stories, and a few short questions.

We are looking for families with a child aged 2 to 5 to run Loomi at bedtime for three weeks, and tell us what actually happened.

Everyone starts together on 5 October 2026.

What we ask

  • One story a night, for fourteen nights.
  • Three weeks from first evening to last. The first three evenings and the last three carry a few questions but no story, so we can see what bedtime looks like either side of the fourteen.
  • A few short questions after each story. Fifteen seconds, and they can wait until the morning.
  • Everyone begins on the same evening, so the nights line up across the group.

What you get

  • All of Loomi unlocked for the three weeks, at no cost.
  • Your free trial stays untouched. If you have not started it, it is still there for you when the pilot ends.
  • A thank-you for families who listen on enough nights.

Missing a night doesn't end anything. We'll just pick up the next evening.

We're running these in small groups so we can read every answer properly. If this round has filled by the time you apply, we'll say so on the spot and write to you first when the next group opens.
